# Claims contract

StackShift measures behavior on a customer's corpus; it does not guarantee outcomes. Decision thresholds are provisional until calibrated and must include provenance.

Allowed historical context: the Drift-Adapter paper reported approximately 95–99% same-family open-model neighbor recovery. StackShift must reproduce and document its own results before publishing performance figures.

Never describe neighbor overlap or synthetic-query output as retrieval quality. StackShift defers an index rebuild; it does not remove that future work.
